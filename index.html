Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this instant online pool calculator? +

Our estimator is calibrated every quarter with live material costs for gunite, rebar, pumps, and pavers in North Texas. The resulting estimate is typically within 5% to 8% of the final engineering quote, assuming standard soil conditions and backyard access.

How long does the entire pool construction take? +

Permitting and HOA approval usually takes 2 to 4 weeks. Once excavation breaks ground, a fiberglass pool takes 3 to 5 weeks, while a custom gunite concrete pool typically takes 6 to 10 weeks depending on custom water features and weather.

Yes! We partner with Lyon Financial and HFS Financial to offer fixed-rate pool loans up to $150,000 with repayment terms extending up to 15 to 20 years. Most homeowners finance their custom oasis for $450 to $750/month.

100%. We handle complete architectural drawings, engineering stamps, municipal permit filing, and HOA submittals so you never have to deal with city bureaucracy.
